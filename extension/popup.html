What connecting does

While you're signed in to Canvas in this browser, Homework Hatch reads the classes you're enrolled in and sends them to your Homework Hatch account about every hour. That includes:

  • your Canvas name and user ID;
  • assignments, due dates, grades, rubrics, comments on your work, and the names of files you turned in;
  • the syllabus, modules, pages and announcements;
  • discussion and quiz titles;
  • calendar events, and your to-do and missing-work lists;
  • each class's file list (names and sizes).

Copies of course files are sent only for the classes you choose on the website. It only reads the Canvas site you connect and never sees your password. You can stop it any time by removing the extension.
