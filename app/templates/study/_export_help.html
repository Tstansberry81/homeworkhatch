{# How to get your OWN set out of Quizlet or Anki. #}

From Quizlet

  1. On the Quizlet website, open a set you made.
  2. Click ••• (more), then Export.
  3. Click Copy text and paste it here.

Leave Quizlet's separators on Tab and New line. Definitions that run over several lines come through fine.

From Anki

  1. In Anki, File → Export.
  2. Pick Notes in Plain Text (.txt) and the deck.
  3. Open the file and copy everything{% if with_file %}, or choose the file below{% endif %}.

Tags are left out and formatting becomes plain text. Each cloze blank becomes its own card.
