How this works

We split your synced grades in two. Test grade counts only tests, quizzes, midterms and finals you took. Everything else is all the other graded work. Missing work shows up as points lost instead of as a low test score. Each item in the table below shows which side it counts on; tap its label to change it. Both numbers count toward your grade, and nothing here is sent to Canvas or your teacher.
