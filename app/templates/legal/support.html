Setting up

  1. Add the Homework Hatch extension to Chrome. It opens the Connect Canvas page.
  2. Sign in (or sign up). The extension links itself to your account on that page.
  3. Open your school's Canvas, click the Homework Hatch icon in the toolbar and press Connect, then allow access.

From then on it syncs every hour while Chrome is open, and only downloads files it doesn't already have.

Common problems

  • "Logged out": log in to Canvas in any tab; the next sync picks up from there. Keeping a Canvas tab open helps at schools with strict sign-in.
  • "Not linked to Homework Hatch yet": open Connect Canvas while signed in.
  • "A file couldn't be uploaded": it's retried automatically at the next sync.
  • A class is missing: the popup's Courses shown list controls which classes sync; it starts with your Canvas dashboard.
  • "Hidden by instructors": normal. Some classes turn off tabs like Files or Pages; the extension reads everything else, including files linked from modules and pages.

Your data

Export or delete everything from Settings → Your data. Revoking the extension's token on the Connect Canvas page stops syncing immediately. See the Privacy Policy.
