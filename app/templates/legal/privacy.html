Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. Template policy; review before relying on it commercially.

What we collect

  • Account data: email, username, display name, password (stored only as a secure hash), time zone, and optional grade level and birth year.
  • Academic data you sync: courses, assignments, submissions, grades, rubric feedback, pages, announcements, and course files, read through the extension under your own Canvas login. We never receive your school password.
  • What you create: flashcards, quizzes, tutor conversations, chat messages, and files you upload or pick from Google Drive.
  • Usage data: sign-ins, syncs, and AI usage counts, to run and improve the Service.

How we use it

Only to provide the Service: showing your coursework, planning, generating study materials, and answering tutor questions. When you use AI features, the relevant course material and your question are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce a response.

Who sees it

Your academic data is private to you. We don't share it with teachers, schools, parents, or advertisers, and we don't sell it. Class chat messages are visible to classmates enrolled in the same Canvas course. Leaderboards show your display name only if you opt in.

Your choices

You can export all your data or permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Your data. Revoking the extension's token stops syncing immediately.

Students and minors

The Service is for users 13+. Users under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent. We follow FERPA and GDPR principles where applicable.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, tokens are stored hashed, and access to your data requires your login or your personal sync token.
