5a. Sharing flashcards and quizzes

Your flashcard decks and practice quizzes are private unless you share one.   • other people's personal information.

To help, when you share, edit or host a set live, we automatically hold back cards the AI wrote from your class files or uploads, and cards you saved from someone else's set, until you rewrite them in your own words, and cards or questions that copy the class materials you've synced to Homework Hatch word for word (ten or more words in a row). When you save someone else's set, you get only what they shared, for your own studying and live games; you can't share it again until you've rewritten it in your own words. We record which shared set each copy came from, so we can remove copies, and live games made from them, when a set is taken down.

Signed-in users can report a shared set. A set reported for copyright or exam content is hidden at once until we review it; for other reasons, once two people report it. A set with a report we haven't reviewed can't be deleted until we do (deleting your whole account still deletes it). If we uphold a report, we take the set down for good, along with any live game made from it, delete the copies people saved from it, and count a strike against the account that shared it. Sets that are taken down or waiting for review, and copies and live games that came from them, can't be hosted live. After three strikes an account can no longer share or host live games, and we may close accounts that keep breaking these rules.
