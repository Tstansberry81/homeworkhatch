Set it up (about 2 minutes)

  1. Download the extension. Download .zip
    Unzip it somewhere you'll keep it (e.g. your Documents folder).
  2. Load it in Chrome (or Edge, Brave, Arc): go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Connect your school: open your Canvas in a tab, click the Homework Hatch icon, then Connect.
  4. Link it to your account: in the extension's Settings, set Homework Hatch server to
    {{ server_url }}
    and paste a server token from the box on the right. Then click Sync now.

After that it syncs every hour while Chrome is open, including new files, grades and announcements. Hidden tabs, locked files and outside tools (WebAssign, Gradescope…) can't be read.
